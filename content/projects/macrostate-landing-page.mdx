# Project Overview

Macro State needed a dedicated solution page to serve as a key step in their sales funnel—where visitors could explore their UniFi Door Access systems in depth after landing on the homepage. The goal was to combine clarity, visual appeal, and conversion optimization within a single, well-structured Webflow page.

# The Solution

I started by creating a Figma mockup to align the design direction with the client’s brand. Once approved, I built the page in Webflow, incorporating:

- A **CMS-based modal system** in the UniFi Hardware section for scalable content updates.
- **Subtle, smooth animations** and hover effects to enhance user experience.
- **Interactive content switching** for the Access Types section, triggered by hover or click.
- **JavaScript enhancements** for accessibility, including Escape-key modal close and background click detection.
- Full **responsive optimization** across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

# The Result

The final page delivered a clean, modern design that effectively communicates Macro State’s offerings while driving conversions. It’s fully interactive, accessible, and easy to manage—creating a professional digital experience that elevates the brand and supports their sales goals.
