# Project Overview

FlexiBank is a mobile banking app built to help users manage their finances digitally without needing to visit a physical branch. The goal was to create an intuitive and accessible platform for users—especially small business owners—who face mobility or location challenges. The app enables users to open and manage accounts, transfer money, track expenses, and secure transactions from anywhere, anytime.

<Figure src="/media/projects/flexibank---online-banking-mobile-app/02-project-overview-min.jpg" alt="FlexiBank screenshot" width={2800} height={1752} />

# Problem Statement

Adamu Ibrahim, a 42-year-old small business owner in Kaduna, Nigeria, struggles with accessing physical bank branches due to mobility and distance limitations. Traditional banking systems are slow, inaccessible, and often require in-person verification. He needs a reliable, easy-to-use mobile banking solution that allows him to manage his finances remote

<Figure src="/media/projects/flexibank---online-banking-mobile-app/03-user-persona-min.jpg" alt="FlexiBank screenshot" width={2800} height={2636} />

# Goal Statement

To design a secure, intuitive, and accessible digital banking experience that simplifies everyday financial tasks. The solution aims to let users open accounts, send and receive money, manage cards, and track expenses—all within a simple, trustworthy interface.

# Research & Insights

I analyzed several leading mobile banking apps such as Kuda, Opay, and GTBank to identify common pain points like complex onboarding and poor accessibility.<br />Through user research, I discovered that:

- Users prefer **biometric login** for faster access.
- Long sign-up forms discourage new users.
- Small business owners want **clear expense tracking** and **secure transfers**.

These insights guided my design decisions throughout the process.

# Design Process

1️⃣ **Empathize** – Conducted user research and identified accessibility challenges.<br />2️⃣ **Define** – Mapped user flows and pain points to clarify priorities.<br />3️⃣ **Ideate** – Sketched and brainstormed low-fidelity wireframes.<br />4️⃣ **Prototype** – Designed high-fidelity screens using a modern, dark UI style.<br />5️⃣ **Test** – Evaluated usability and refined key user journeys for simplicity.

# The Solution

The final design delivers a user-friendly and secure experience.<br />Key features include:

- Quick account setup with guided onboarding
- Biometric authentication for secure login
- Simplified money transfers and bill payments
- Expense tracking with categorized insights
- Clean, modern dark UI for comfort and focus

# The Result

The project resulted in a complete UX case study demonstrating the full process—from user research to final prototype. FlexiBank successfully meets the goal of creating a **secure, accessible, and efficient banking experience**, tailored for users in Nigeria who need reliable financial tools on the go.
