# Project Overview

The goal was to elevate Lisa Prezer’s brand with a high-end website that reflects beauty, elegance, and trust. It needed multiple sections including services, portfolio, booking, customer reviews, and password-protected training content — all consistent with her refined brand identity.

# The Solution

I crafted a modern, airy design using neutral tones, serif typography, and subtle silver accents to achieve a “luxury spa” aesthetic. The structure included a CMS-based portfolio, pricing lists, and dedicated training pages, all localized in German using Figma + Webflow tools for seamless translation and scalability.

# The Result

A visually cohesive and responsive Webflow website that communicates elegance and credibility while remaining easy for the client to manage. Lisa and David both praised the project for its premium aesthetic, brand alignment, and professional execution.
